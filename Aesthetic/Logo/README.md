# Open Folio identity

Approved October 4, 2026 for Open Resume Toolkit. Two document leaves share an open spine; the raised right leaf gives the mark forward direction.

Use `open-folio-mark.svg` on light surfaces and `open-folio-reversed.svg` in the deep teal navigation rail. Use `open-folio-lockup.svg` for the complete product name, and `open-folio-app.svg` or `app-1024.png` for the application icon. macOS and Windows bundles are `OpenFolio.icns` and `OpenFolio.ico`. Logo letters are outlines and require no installed font.

The app and extension generated icons use this same master. Exported resumes and cover letters do not contain the mark or inherit the application colors. Prior Open Frame masters are archived in `../previous refrence/Logo`.
