# Open Resume Toolkit additional attribution term

## Relationship to GPLv3

Unless a file or component states otherwise, original Open Resume Toolkit
material is licensed under the GNU General Public License version 3 only
(`GPL-3.0-only`). The official, unmodified GPLv3 text is in `LICENSE`.

For material whose copyright holder has applied this term, the following is an
additional term permitted by Section 7(b) of GPLv3. It does not replace or
modify the GPL text.

## Applicability notice

Xavier Reid applies this term to original Open Resume Toolkit material for
which he holds copyright and which he distributes through the canonical source
repository, unless the material states otherwise. Material contributed by
another copyright holder is covered only when that holder explicitly applies
this term.

Each original program source file added after adoption of this term must carry
a copyright notice, an appropriate `GPL-3.0-only` license identifier, and a
notice that `ADDITIONAL_TERMS.md` applies. Generated files and formats that do
not support comments may point to these notices through their documented source
or distribution metadata.

## Required attribution

Anyone who conveys verbatim or modified copies of material covered by this
term must preserve the following reasonable author attribution:

> Contains or is based on Open Resume Toolkit. Original source:
> https://github.com/xjreid/Open-Resume-Toolkit

The attribution must appear:

1. In the `NOTICE` file, or a reasonably equivalent source-distribution notice;
   and
2. If the distributed work provides an About, Credits, or legal-notices view,
   in that view or in a clearly linked legal-notices page.

The notice may be translated or reformatted, but the project name, the meaning
that the work contains or is based on Open Resume Toolkit, and the canonical
repository URL must remain clear. A modified work must not imply that its
changes came from or were endorsed by the Open Resume Toolkit project.

## Scope

This term applies only to material for which the relevant copyright holder has
placed a notice in the material, or a notice indicating that this file applies.
It does not extend to independent works merely distributed alongside Open
Resume Toolkit or to third-party material under separate terms.

This term does not restrict charging money, commercial use, offering paid
services, modification, or redistribution otherwise permitted by GPLv3.

The Open Resume Toolkit name and branding remain subject to `TRADEMARKS.md`.
Preserving the attribution does not make a modified or third-party build an
official release and does not grant trademark rights.
