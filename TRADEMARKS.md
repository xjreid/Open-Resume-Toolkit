# Open Resume Toolkit name and trademark policy

## Purpose

This policy helps users distinguish official Open Resume Toolkit releases from
modified or third-party distributions. It governs names and branding, not the
copyright permissions granted for the software under GPLv3.

“Open Resume Toolkit,” “ORT,” project logos, and distinctive project branding
are referred to below as the project marks. This policy does not represent that
any mark is registered in any jurisdiction.

## Uses that do not require permission

You may use the project name accurately to:

- Refer to or link to the Open Resume Toolkit project.
- State that software is compatible with Open Resume Toolkit.
- State that a modified work is based on, derived from, or forked from Open
  Resume Toolkit.
- Redistribute an unmodified official release while accurately identifying its
  source and preserving all required notices.
- Discuss, review, teach, or report on the project.

These uses must not imply sponsorship, endorsement, or official status when
none exists.

## Modified and third-party distributions

A materially modified distribution must use a distinct primary product name
and must identify itself as unofficial. It may use wording such as “based on
Open Resume Toolkit” to provide the attribution required by
`ADDITIONAL_TERMS.md`.

Without prior written permission, a modified or third-party distribution must
not:

- Use Open Resume Toolkit or ORT as its primary product, company, service,
  package, domain, or publisher name.
- Use project logos or branding in a way likely to make the distribution appear
  official.
- Describe itself as approved, certified, sponsored, maintained, or published
  by the Open Resume Toolkit project.
- Use official signing identities, update metadata, package identifiers,
  browser-extension identifiers, or release channels.

Forks should use distinct package identifiers, update endpoints, signing
identities, and browser-extension/native-host identifiers so that they cannot
overwrite or impersonate official installations.

## Official status and permission

Only releases published through channels identified by the project as official
may describe themselves as official Open Resume Toolkit releases. Permission
for uses outside this policy must be obtained in writing from the applicable
mark owner through a public project contact method.

The project may revise this policy as its branding, ownership, or registration
status develops. A revision does not retroactively change software copyright
permissions already granted under GPLv3.
